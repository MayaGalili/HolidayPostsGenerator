Title: AgroScout - Leaf-Level AI Pest and Disease Detection
Category: Software Platform
Content:
AgroScout's Pest & Disease Detection solution is designed to address early-stage crop infection and infestation risks by utilizing deep learning algorithms to scan high-resolution imagery down to the leaf level. Traditional manual crop scouting often fails to detect pathogens until visible symptoms affect 10% to 15% of a field, leading to reactive chemical applications. AgroScout's proprietary AI models process drone and imagery inputs to identify biological threats, such as late blight, early blight, alternaria leaf spot, and Colorado potato beetle, long before widespread outbreak occurs. The platform automatically assigns severity scoring (high, medium, or low risk ratings) to specific field zones and provides GPS-tagged plant location data. This spatial precision enables agronomists and growers to move directly to flagged problem areas, generate targeted prescription maps, and perform variable-rate localized spraying instead of applying blanket chemical treatments. Key keywords: precision agriculture, AI pest detection, leaf-level crop scouting, deep learning agronomy, plant disease identification, severity scoring, targeted chemical treatment.
