Title: AgroScout - Case Study: Targeted Crop Protection in Potato Production
Category: Case Study
Content:
AgroScout's Pest & Disease Detection technology was utilized by potato growers facing severe seasonal late blight pressure, an aggressive pathogen that typically leads to heavy yield destruction and blanket chemical applications. To prevent significant crop loss, growers used AgroScout's AI-driven visual scouting tools to scan 100% of their cultivated field area from the air. AgroScout identified early infection sites down to individual leaves, allowing farm managers to deploy targeted spot applications to flagged infection zones instead of spraying entire fields. As a result, the operation achieved a 17% reduction in total fungicide usage, saving $120 per hectare in input costs. Furthermore, early intervention preserved marketable tuber yields at 21.5 metric tons per hectare—significantly outperforming the regional outbreak average of 12 metric tons per hectare—yielding a 1:8 return on investment across the season. Key keywords: potato crop protection, late blight control, targeted spraying case study, pesticide reduction, precision yield protection, agronomic ROI.
