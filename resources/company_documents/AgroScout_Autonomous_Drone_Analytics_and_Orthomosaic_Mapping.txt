Title: AgroScout - Autonomous Drone Analytics and Orthomosaic Mapping
Category: Drone Analytics
Content:
AgroScout's Drone Analytics technology is designed to address incomplete field coverage and time-consuming manual sampling by using autonomous drone flight paths and computer vision processing. Operating through mobile flight apps, AgroScout enables off-the-shelf standard drones to automatically capture high-resolution RGB photographs across targeted field grids. The cloud system stitches these images into centimeter-accurate orthomosaic maps while simultaneously passing sub-images through computer vision detection pipelines. This capability supports precise early-stage agronomic assessments, including automated plant stand counts approximately 14 days after emergence to evaluate replant decisions, as well as ongoing canopy coverage analysis to evaluate crop vigor and closure. By turning raw aerial photography into geotagged data, AgroScout eliminates guessing in field surveys and drastically reduces total scouting time. Key keywords: autonomous drone scouting, orthomosaic mapping, stand count analysis, canopy coverage, computer vision, early emergence evaluation, aerial image processing.
