Title: AgroScout - Case Study: Satellite Analytics for Flood Claim Verification
Category: Case Study
Content:
AgroScout's Satellite Analytics platform was deployed to address a dispute regarding widespread flood damage claims across 1,300 hectares of commercial wheat fields. An agricultural insurance company required an objective, remote method to verify field damage without sending manual adjusters across flooded terrain. Using AgroScout's multi-spectral satellite monitoring software, the insurer conducted a historical and post-event analysis across the entire 1,300-hectare area to measure actual vegetation indices and standing water impact. The satellite analytics revealed that the actual affected crop area was only 147 hectares, compared to initial grower claims of 545 hectares. This verified spatial assessment saved the insurer $265,450 in payout claims while providing a 1:235 return on investment for the analytical service, demonstrating the accuracy of AgroScout's remote sensing infrastructure. Key keywords: agricultural insurance case study, satellite damage verification, wheat crop analytics, objective risk assessment, remote sensing claim audit.
